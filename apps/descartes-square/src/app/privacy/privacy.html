Privacy

What we store, and what leaves this app

Short version: your squares are private to your account, and the only thing that leaves this app is the text you ask us to get AI suggestions for.

What we store

Your email address and username, so you can sign in. The decisions you write — the title, the four quadrants, your confidence and your conclusion. Nothing else.

AI suggestions and Google Gemini

When you ask for suggestions on a quadrant, the decision title and that quadrant's arguments are sent to Google Gemini to generate them. If you never use AI suggestions, nothing you write leaves this app. We don't send your email, username or conclusions.

Who can see your squares

Only you. There is no sharing or public-link feature, so a square is visible to the account that created it and to nobody else.
